## Overview

Designed a four-layer conceptual framework for multi-hop explainable AI in clinical decision support and implemented it as System C — a 3-hop reasoning pipeline for identification, confirmation, and management.

The system was verified against a guideline knowledge base spanning six clinical conditions, achieving 91% guideline-consistency verification, 100% rejection of out-of-scope cases, and 100% discrimination on a controlled ground-truth test across 56 real clinical notes. Its reliability was also validated through a 36-rater human evaluation focused on clarity and trust.

## Tools

Python, LLM-based reasoning and verification, ChromaDB, Sentence-Transformers, RAG pipeline design
